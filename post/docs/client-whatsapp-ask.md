# Client kickoff — the ask (WhatsApp-ready), pre-production only

The pre-production kit. Copy-paste into WhatsApp; asterisks render as bold on the phone. The post-production ask (footage, audio files, post intake decisions) is a separate message sent after Gate A.

---

Hi team 👋 — to run your project neatly for pre-production, here's the kit we need from your side. Doing it upfront saves us stopping and asking mid-run.

📄 *1. Brief & concept*
• Brief: what you want, target length, aspect (16:9 / 9:16 / 1:1 / 4:5), language + subtitle rule, platform, hard deadline
• *One* concept doc marked as the base (if there's a draft and a rework, tell us which wins)
• Email thread with client input — save as PDF and drop in

🎨 *2. Brand + references*
• Logo (transparent PNG or SVG), brand colours (hex), product shots
• Any face or place we must match (staff, real locations, cast) — location images help
• 3–5 look references — images or clips showing the visual style you want
• Sites we can research references from (Vimeo staff picks, brand X's YouTube, Behance boards, etc.) — a short list is enough

📊 *3. Your templates* (once per client, your own .xlsx files)
• Budget sheet
• Timeline sheet
• Concept breakdown (your MOT format)
• Call sheet (your layout)
We fill your shape, not a generic one.

⚙️ *4. Kickoff decisions* (5 quick calls at intake)
• Script format: screenplay or AV script
• Storyboard style: sketches / live pictures / cartoon (pick one)
• Live shoot with shoot days, or fully AI-generated
• Cast facts: ages, names, relations
• Locale: which city, real place or generic build

🧾 *5. Logistics you'll enter on the board* (after the creative lock, needed before the logistics lock)
• Talents — per role: name, picture, age, availability, cost, loading
• Key props — name, which scene, where it comes from, do we have it
• Locations — name, address, availability, contact person
Anything that truly doesn't apply, mark it "not applicable" on the board — that's a decision, a blank is not.
Voice, music and sound effects we produce in-house; you'll pick from options on the board. Only send a track if there's one you insist on.

📁 *6. Where it goes*
• Project Drive folder with `Brief/`, `Concept/`, `Client Assets/`, plus a `sites.md` or `links.txt` for section 2's research sites

👥 *7. Named approvers* (three seats — each gate waits on one)
• *Creative director* — locks script, storyboard, shot list
• *Production assistant* — enters talents / props / locations
• *Production lead* — releases the call sheets

⏱️ *8. Time & responsiveness*
• Expect 4 approval stops: the sample storyboard panel, then Gate A (creative lock), Gate B (logistics lock), Gate C (document release, one per shoot day)
• Each stop is you saying "yes" or "change X" — on the board or in this chat
• Someone reachable to answer the inbox during the run; an unanswered question stalls us

💰 *9. Sorted on our side*
• 3echo credits for the storyboard panels (one per panel, one per redo — you press Generate and Redo on the board yourself)
• The board (a live dashboard we send you) for approvals, questions and comments

(If any of the above you'd prefer the AI to do instead, tell us — we'll flag it as a hole, not a guess.)

🔁 *One more thing*
• If you have a project you've already run start to end the traditional way, we'd rather test on that one — same brief, same assets — so we can put the two side by side afterwards and compare quality, time and cost honestly.

---

## Copy variants

**Short version (single WhatsApp message)**

> Pre-prod kit we need: brief + one concept doc marked as base, brand assets (logo/colours/products/references) and a few sites to research from, your 4 templates (budget/timeline/breakdown/call sheet), 5 kickoff decisions (script format, storyboard style, shoot vs AI, cast facts, locale), and 3 named approvers (creative director, production assistant, production lead). After the creative lock you'll enter talents, props and locations on the board; voice, music and SFX we produce in-house. Ideally give us a project you've already completed so we can compare the two afterwards. Anything missing = tell us, we flag as a hole not a guess.

**One-line ask (if they just want the headline)**

> Send us: brief, concept, brand assets, references + research sites, your 4 templates, cast + locale facts, and 3 named approvers — ideally on a project you've already run, so we can compare afterwards.
