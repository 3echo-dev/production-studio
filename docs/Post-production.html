01

What enters post

Two arrivals converge in the intake bay: the release package from the pre-production plugin (the paper trail) and the crew's shoot folder (the physical output). Intake refuses to advance if either side is missing.

From pre-prod release

Gate C · closed
  • script.md, final approved
  • storyboard.json, panel table with frame refs
  • concept-breakdown.xlsx, talent, prop and location line items
  • call-sheet-day-*.pdf, one per shoot day
  • storyboard/v{n}/P*.png, the visual panels

Bound by hash to the pre-prod release. A change upstream reopens post at intake.

From the shoot

Drive folder
  • footage/day-*/A001_C*.mov, camera masters
  • footage/day-*/proxies/, editing proxies (optional)
  • audio/lav/, talent lavalier tracks
  • audio/boom/, boom capture
  • audio/ambient/, room tone, wild lines
  • slates/, clapper reference frames

Ingest computes checksums on every file. A missing take is a question, not a silent skip.

Human declarations

XX items
  • Aspect ratio target (16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5, 2.39:1)
  • Deliverable duration (30 s, 60 s, cutdowns)
  • Reference cut, if one exists
  • Colour direction, LUT or grade note; for 3echo, brand-kit section 5
  • Audio hero: VO-led or picture-led

The pipeline never fills an XX for you. A blank stays a question.

03

Agents · ten seats

Seven doing production work, three coordinating. Same convention as pre-prod: each director wraps one or two skills and writes one artifact; the orchestrator only walks the table, verifies files on disk, and is the only agent that spends.

00Orchestrator

Walks the 12-state table, checks output artefacts exist, advances state through the two gates, records every version and run on the board.

01Post Intake

Validates the pre-prod release package plus the shoot folder plus the human declarations; binds the release hash into job.json.

02Ingest Director

Reads the shoot folder, catalogs clips and audio, computes checksums, emits the manifest. No agent types a clip path.

03Sequence Director

Frame-matches the storyboard against the manifest, picks best takes, emits the rough EDL.

04BGM Supervisor

Reads script tone and storyboard beats, shortlists three candidates, presents them on the board.

05Audio Engineer

Owns VO drop-in and the sync pass: VO, BGM and SFX aligned to picture, music ducked under voice.

06B-roll Director

Handles on-demand B-roll requests. Saves to the bundle with a slate frame. Does not auto-splice into the cut.

07Mograph Director

Lower thirds, subtitles, reveals and the end card as alpha-channel exports via Remotion, on brand.

08Premiere Packager

Builds the FCPXML timeline and the bundle folder tree for editor handoff.

09Review Director

Runs the combined picture+audio rubric before Gate A. Blocks or clears. Never approves.

04

Skills · fourteen

The leaf-level playbooks each director invokes, in workflow order.

S1

post

Entry command. /creative-studio-post:post starts or resumes a job.

S2

new-post-project

Creates project.json from the shoot path and the pre-prod release path; proposes the client's defaults.

S3

resume-post-project

Reads state and artefacts on disk to resume a stalled job. Never passes a gate.

S4

ingest-footage

ffprobe over every clip and audio file. Writes the manifest with checksums.

S5

sequence-first-cut

Storyboard-to-footage frame match. Emits the rough EDL; falls back to a human EDL without open_clip.

S6

bgm-options

Shortlists three candidates from the supplied tracks; the person picks one on the board. No MCP tool.

S7

vo-drop-in

Places VO takes against script markers on the timeline.

S8

audio-sync

Local ffmpeg sync. Emits the synced WAV and waveform PNG. No MCP tool.

S9

broll-generate

One request, quoted on the board, then create_video_job on AgentC. Saves to the bundle.

S10

mograph-generate

Remotion render for infographic or motion-graphic alphas, one sample before the batch.

S11

export-premiere-bundle

build-bundle.js: Gate A lock check, FCPXML 1.10, README, hash manifest.

S12

board-sync

Push batches to the Post Control board, land decisions, hash-bound gate pull.

S13

review-pass

Runs the rubric against a gate. Emits the review verdict.

S14

board-setup

Publishes this account's own Post Control board and records its address.

05

Tools · what actually does the work

Video and image generation go through the existing 3echo AgentC MCP; every call is against an endpoint pre-prod already uses. Everything else is local execution: ffmpeg, Remotion, python.

Video generation

MCP · 3echo AgentC
  • SeeDance B-roll, main engine
  • 9 image refs per job continuity
  • 3 video refs, 3 audio refs per job

create_video_job on the same AgentC MCP pre-prod uses. Whatever engines AgentC adds later, post inherits.

Image generation

MCP · 3echo AgentC
  • GPT image models stills, plates
  • 16 image refs per job continuity
  • flat 1 credit per image no per-model quote

create_image_job. Slate frames, mograph plate art, continuity thumbnails. Same tool pre-prod's make-image uses.

Audio

MCP · external
  • ElevenLabs scratch VO, transcription
  • Client-supplied WAV BGM, for now

Final VO uses talent takes from the shoot. No music service is wired: the client supplies the files and the supervisor shortlists three.

Sequence retrieval

OM · clip_embedder
  • CLIP ViT-L/14 frame embedding
  • storyboard to footage semantic match
  • best-take ranking per panel

CLIP matching pointed at shoot footage instead of stock libraries. open_clip and PyTorch installed 21 Sep.

Motion graphics

OM · remotion-composer
  • Cinematic titles, reveals
  • Explainer infographic
  • TalkingHead lower thirds
  • CollageBurst, LyricOverlay extras

Templates ship with the 3echo Motion composer. Alpha exports (ProRes 4444) drop into Premiere.

Assembly + sync

Local · ffmpeg
  • ffmpeg transcode, concat, sync
  • ffprobe clip metadata, timecode
  • media_profiles codec presets, OM

Runs on your machine. Sync passes on multi-gigabyte footage do not survive a cloud round-trip.

Gate records

Local · ours
  • record-approval.js verdict to approvals/*.json
  • board-sync.js pull board lock to hash-bound record
  • check-approval.js refuses a changed file

Hash-bound records survive a resume. Editing a file after the lock invalidates it.

Delivery format

Local · python, ours
  • FCPXML 1.10 Premiere-importable
  • build-fcpxml.py EDL + synced track to timeline
  • 33 runtime scripts route, plan, state, records, bundle

Written by us. A rendered MP4 is not enough; an editor needs a Premiere project.

06

Frame vs engine

Where the code comes from. An honest split so we do not rewrite work that already exists, and do not inherit shapes that fight the studio's workflow.

The frame

We build
  • Intake + release-package validation agency-specific
  • 10 director agents match pre-prod
  • 2-gate structure + board-sync team reviews
  • Storyboard-driven sequencing from pre-prod
  • FCPXML packager editor handoff
  • Post Control board sibling to the Pre-production board

The shape agency post actually has. Not autonomous: the editor keeps control.

The engine

OM · reused
  • lib/clip_embedder semantic retrieval
  • remotion-composer 5 mograph templates
  • lib/media_profiles codec awareness

Proven, working code. The footage-repurpose plugin already runs on this pattern.

What we do not lift

OM · skip
  • executive-producer autonomy too hands-off
  • documentary-montage pipeline thematic, not storyboard
  • retrieval from Pexels or NASA we shoot our own
  • delivery renderer to MP4 we need FCPXML
  • checkpoint, delivery_promise present, not called

Other pipelines' opinions fit their use case; they would fight ours. Take the libs, leave the doctrine.

Direct answer

Does it have a locally executed editor?

No, and that is deliberate. The pipeline does not open a video editor of its own. It produces an FCPXML timeline and an asset bundle that Premiere Pro imports in one step, and the human editor finishes the pass there.

What runs locally is the assembly engine: ffmpeg for the sync pass, the 3echo Motion composer for mograph, a CLIP matcher for storyboard-to-footage matching, our own python for the FCPXML build. These do the mechanical work. They do not render a final master.

Why not build an editorPremiere Pro is where the editor's craft lives. Replacing it means competing with three decades of workflow. Wrong fight.
Why FCPXMLPremiere, DaVinci Resolve and Final Cut all import it losslessly. One handoff format, three possible editors, no lock-in.
Why local executionffmpeg and CLIP retrieval on your machine are a hundred times faster than a hosted job on multi-gigabyte footage.
The 70 to 80 percent ruleThe pipeline reaches a rough cut with sync and BGM. The editor picks up from there: punch-ins, slow-mo, colour, titles.
